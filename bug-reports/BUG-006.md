# BUG-006: Shopping Cart Order Total Fails to Reactively Update on Quantity Change

**Severity:** High — Cart state inconsistency displaying outdated total, causing customer confusion at checkout.
**Framework:** Maestro / Appium / XCUITest
**Element Identifier:** `qty-increment-p1`, `qty-decrement-p1`, `order-total`, `line-total-p1`
**Cross-Build Status:** Reproduces on both SwiftUI & React Native
**Blocks Other Tests:** No

### Environment
- **Build Type:** SwiftUI (`com.techshop.ios`) & React Native (Expo)
- **Build Variant:** Broken
- **iOS Version:** iOS 26.5
- **Simulator / Device:** iPhone 17 Pro Simulator
- **Framework & Runner:** Maestro v2.8.0 / pytest 8.4.2 / xcodebuild

### Steps to Reproduce
1. Launch the app fresh on a clean simulator.
2. Log in with `demo@techshop.com` / `password123`.
3. Add Product 1 (Wireless Headphones - $60.00) to the cart and open the Cart tab.
4. Verify initial Order Total is "Order Total: $60" (`order-total`).
5. Tap the increment button `qty-increment-p1` to increase quantity to 2.
6. Observe line total `line-total-p1` and Order Total `order-total`.

### Expected Result
Line total updates to "Line: $120" and the overall Order Total reactively recalculates and immediately updates to display "Order Total: $120".

### Actual Result
Line total displays "Line: $120", but Order Total remains frozen at "Order Total: $60" until an external event forces re-render.
