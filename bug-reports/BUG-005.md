# BUG-005: Shopping Cart Quantity Stepper Decrements Below 1

**Severity:** Medium — Boundary validation failure allowing cart quantity to reach 0 or negative integers.
**Framework:** Maestro / Appium / XCUITest
**Element Identifier:** `qty-decrement-p1`, `qty-p1`, `line-total-p1`, `order-total`
**Cross-Build Status:** Reproduces on both SwiftUI & React Native
**Blocks Other Tests:** No

### Environment
- **Build Type:** SwiftUI (`com.techshop.ios`) & React Native (Expo)
- **Build Variant:** Broken
- **iOS Version:** iOS 26.5
- **Simulator / Device:** iPhone 17 Pro Simulator
- **Framework & Runner:** Maestro v2.8.0 / pytest 8.4.2 / xcodebuild

### Steps to Reproduce
1. Launch the app fresh on a clean simulator.
2. Log in with valid credentials (`demo@techshop.com` / `password123`).
3. Add Product 1 to the cart (`add-p1`) and navigate to the Cart tab.
4. Verify initial quantity is 1 (`qty-p1`).
5. Tap the decrement button `qty-decrement-p1` once.

### Expected Result
Tapping decrement at quantity 1 clamps the value at 1 (or removes the item with prompt). The quantity field `qty-p1` does not decrement below 1.

### Actual Result
Quantity decrements from 1 to 0 (and subsequently to -1 upon additional taps), causing invalid negative cart state.
