# BUG-009: Checkout Payment Form Accepts Expired Credit Card Dates

**Severity:** High — Financial and validation integrity defect allowing expired payment instruments to submit.
**Framework:** Maestro / Appium / XCUITest
**Element Identifier:** `checkout-expiry`, `checkout-submit`, `checkout-error`
**Cross-Build Status:** Reproduces on both SwiftUI & React Native
**Blocks Other Tests:** No (Is blocked by BUG-011 on broken build)

### Environment
- **Build Type:** SwiftUI (`com.techshop.ios`) & React Native (Expo)
- **Build Variant:** Broken / Fixed (Requires BUG-011 fix to reach checkout screen)
- **iOS Version:** iOS 26.5
- **Simulator / Device:** iPhone 17 Pro Simulator
- **Framework & Runner:** Maestro v2.8.0 / pytest 8.4.2 / xcodebuild

### Steps to Reproduce
*(Note: Because BUG-011 makes 'Proceed to Checkout' a no-op on the initial broken build, test with BUG-011 fix applied or by navigating directly to the Checkout screen).*

1. Launch the app fresh on a clean simulator.
2. Log in with `demo@techshop.com` / `password123`.
3. Add Product 1 ($60) to cart by tapping `add-p1` and open the Cart tab.
4. Tap "Proceed to Checkout" (`proceed-checkout`) to navigate to Checkout.
5. In `checkout-firstName`, enter `Jane`.
6. In `checkout-lastName`, enter `Doe`.
7. In `checkout-email`, enter `jane.doe@example.com`.
8. In `checkout-phone`, enter `1234567890`.
9. In `checkout-card`, enter `1111222233334444`.
10. In `checkout-expiry`, enter expired past date `01/20`.
11. In `checkout-cvv`, enter `123`.
12. Tap "Place Order" (`checkout-submit`).

### Expected Result
Submission is rejected. The user remains on the Checkout screen and error message "Expiry date must not be in the past" is displayed via `checkout-error`.

### Actual Result
Past expiration date `01/20` is accepted without validation, placing the order and navigating to the Confirmation screen.
